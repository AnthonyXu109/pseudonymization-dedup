# Follow-up experiments v9 (written 2026-10-06 before running)

Reviewer requests: natural cross-source duplicates, and an embedding-based (semantic) deduplication baseline.
Exploratory, prompted by reviews; all results reported.

## E4. Natural cross-source duplicates: the same ECtHR judgment in TAB and in LexGLUE
TAB (from HUDOC, procedure and facts sections, one text) and LexGLUE ECtHR (facts paragraphs joined by newlines)
publish many of the same judgments with different extraction and formatting. Matching (done before any pseudonymization
analysis): for each TAB judgment, the LexGLUE document with the most shared sampled shingles; a pair is kept if at least
80% of the LexGLUE document's word 5-grams occur in the TAB document (containment >= 0.8). Matching used raw text only.
Both sides marked by the same spaCy en_core_web_lg detector (cached spans from the main runs). Strategies RAW, MASK,
SUR-CORPUS, SUR-DOC, HASH-ENTITY, each document pseudonymized separately. Metric: pair recall at theta = 0.5 and 0.7
(exact word-5-gram Jaccard) and median m_doc of the TAB side. Expectation stated before running: SUR-DOC and HASH-ENTITY
lose most pairs, SUR-CORPUS stays near RAW.

## E5. Embedding-based duplicate detection
Question: does an embedding-similarity deduplicator lose copies under document-scoped pseudonyms?
Embeddings: (a) mean-pooled last-layer states of RoBERTa-base from spaCy en_core_web_trf 3.8 on the first 300 words of
each text (truncation, as embedding deduplication commonly embeds a bounded prefix); (b) average static word vectors of
en_core_web_lg on the full text.
Samples: 300 ECtHR second-source pairs, 500 web second-source pairs, 300 natural cross-source pairs from E4 (fixed
random samples, seed 20261006). Strategies RAW, MASK, SUR-DOC.
Decision rule: cosine similarity >= tau, where tau is set per strategy and dataset so that 0.1% of random pairs of
different originals (all pairs among the sampled originals) exceed it. Metric: recall of true pairs at that tau, plus the
median cosine of true pairs.

Outputs: results/v9/e4_natural.json, results/v9/e5_embed.json. Script: exp_v9.py.

## E5b addendum (written 2026-10-06 01:35, after seeing the ECtHR SUR-DOC rows of E5, before running E5b)
The SUR-DOC pseudonyms (`zq` + 10 letters) are out of vocabulary for en_core_web_lg, so the static-vector embedder
gives them zero vectors and in effect ignores them. This is a property of the token format, not of document scoping.
To check whether static vectors also ignore realistic or hash pseudonyms, E5b repeats E5 on the same 300 ECtHR pairs
(same seed and sampling code) with FAKER-DOC and HASH-ENTITY. Post hoc; reported whatever the result.
Output: results/v9/e5b_embed.json.
